## The Ultimate Gadget

When I was a young I was obsessed with gadgets. I loved James Bond's communicator watch, Batman's grappling hook, flying cars, lightsabers, you name it. They were little devices that gave you superpowers. Some of my first visits on YouTube were "Jetpack real life", or "Smoke bomb real life." I would pan through hours of failed experiments, and clickbate thumbnails, each video giving me little tastes of the future. Knowing this, imagine how excited I was when smartphones first debued, and then smartwatches! They were little bricks of glass and silicoln that could do just about anything. They truely seemed like the ultimate gadget. 

As the size of the App Store increased, as IOT devices became popular, and as more and more superpowers became available, I was always at the forfront. Fast forward a couple years, and I still like to think I'm at the forfront. I just watched Unbox Therapy's review of the Even G2 Smartglasses, and I really had to pull myself away from that 'Add to Cart' button. Something different is now though. After all this time curating my perfect collection of superpowers, I can sit on the couch all day, wave around my gadgets, and I'll likely be able to ensure my survival for the forseeable future. You would think maybe my younger self would be impressed, right? Is this the life I imagined for myself? Probably not. I think the reason I was so obsessed with gadgets wasn't becuase they gave me superpowers, *it's because they made me feel like a superhero.* And (while you probably don't need me to tell you this) if you're wondering whether or not sitting on the couch all day makes you feel like a super hero, *it does not.*
