This article is a list of tips and practices that have helped me to live life.

## Don't multitask
There are absolutely times when multitasking is necessary, but try to minimize them as much as possible. *Especially* in your personal time. 
If your evening commute consists of recording voice notes while dodging E-bikes, with music is blasting in the background, you're probably not able to appreciate the setting sun.
If your Saturday morning routine consists of, watching AMTA/Minecraft parkour on your phone, while eating, with Parks and Rec playing in the background, you're probably barely registering notes of hazlenut the coffee you just brewed.
