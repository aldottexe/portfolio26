200kmi is a website with one goal: to give non-car enthusiests the information they need to take care of their cars. Inspiration for this website stems from my own past experiences owning cars, and things that I've learned on my  own journey to form a better, more informed relationship with my car. You can View the full case study [here](https://medium.com/tylerdi/200-000-miles-resurrecting-the-driver-car-relationship-e43a7d75f9c5).

![4](/projects/200kmi/4.png)

## Process
My main constraint for this project was time. I only had about 3 months from concept to finish. I knew it would be impossible to create a comprehensive guide to car mechanics and maintenance in that amount of time. Especially since this was just before AI agents had become commonplace. Instead of rushing the project or downsizing, I speant most of the 3 months focusing on architecture. I built a robust dynamic routing system that would automatically style page content link it to respective 3d models by name. While AI agents weren't yet a thing, the ChatGPT API did exist. I wrote a simple script that would loop over all pages that needed content, and prompted ChatGPT to fill in the blanks.
