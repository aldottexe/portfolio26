# 200kmi
200kmi is a website with one simple goal: give non-car enthusiests the information they need to take care of their cars. Inspiration for this website stems from my own past experiences owning cars, and things that I've learned recently on a journey to form a better, more informed relationship with my car. You can View the full case study [here](https://medium.com/tylerdi/200-000-miles-resurrecting-the-driver-car-relationship-e43a7d75f9c5).
