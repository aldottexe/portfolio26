## Goal
Create a strategy card game created to mimicked the feel of a Hollywood hacking scene.

![hello](/hackoff/greenfw.png)

## Developement
The concept was to create cards and a game box representing a box of hard drives modified by some hacker to suit their needs.
The design of the cards utilized a mix of 3d rendering and hand written text.
