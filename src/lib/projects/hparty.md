## Purpose
HParty is a companion web app for the tabletop rollplaying game Dungeons & Dragons. It enables users to track and share their current game statistics with their teammates and spectators. Supabase Realtime allows for cloud data storage, persistance in between gaming sessions, and realtime updates through websockets.
