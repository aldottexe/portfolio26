Lamy is a company that, in my opinion, makes beautiful products. They also tailor these products to a wide consumerbase ranging from high-end collectors, to artists, to school children. When taking taking these values into account, I set 3 goals for this project.
1. Do not compete with or detract from Lamy's product design.
2. Portray an element of playfulness that reflects the creativity and self expression their products enable.
3. Reflect the respect and prestige of the brand.
