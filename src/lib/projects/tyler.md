## About the Project
I worked under Abby Guido on the Tyler School of Art and Architecture’s website redesign, helping redevelop the school’s online visual identity. Under her art direction, I created a set of illustrations and icons, along with a framework for others to replicate them consistently. By the end of the project, we shipped an icon pack and style guide that are still in use across Tyler’s site today.

The project followed two years working as a designer for the school. During that time, I produced print and digital assets for departmental use, public-facing events, and social media. This project particularly pushed my to find creative solutions whithin a very defined brand, and to create in a way that was equally defined, and replicable.
